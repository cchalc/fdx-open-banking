## 1. What the CDBA framework requires and its phases

### Legal basis
- Consumer-Driven Banking Act, enacted through Bill C-15, Royal Assent 2026-03-26 (see section 0). [V]
- Under the framework, consumers can direct participating entities to share financial data. Participants must implement a single technical standard, described as "a set of specifications for the APIs used to share data." [V] https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/programs/financial-sector-policy/open-banking-implementation/budget-2025-canadas-framework-for-consumer-driven-banking.html
- Participants that outsource consent, authentication or data movement must use an accredited third-party provider and remain liable for their obligations. [V] (same Budget 2025 page)
- Accreditation requires a national-security screen and ongoing mandatory reporting. [V] (same page)
- Five entry pathways: mandated banks; federally or provincially regulated financial institutions (streamlined); RPAA-registered payment service providers (streamlined); all other entities such as fintechs; and third-party service providers such as aggregators. [V] (same page)

### Regulations
- Proposed Consumer-Driven Banking Regulations were pre-published on 2026-06-26, with the Canada Gazette Part I dated 2026-06-27. [V] https://www.canada.ca/en/department-finance/news/2026/06/government-pre-publishes-regulations-to-prevent-fraud-and-facilitate-the-next-phase-of-consumer-driven-banking.html ; https://www.bankofcanada.ca/core-functions/consumer-driven-banking/
- Coming into force is staggered "beginning with accreditation." Common rules and assessment fees follow "within one year of final publication." Product and service details "will be set out when the final regulations are published." [V] (June 2026 release)
- I found no evidence in accessible sources that final regulations have been published as of 2026-11-26. [U]

## 4. Open questions

### (a) Has the FDX Canada Council published Canada-specific deltas to v6?

**Answer: Not as far as I can find.** [V]

- FDX Canada says key Canadian technical decisions "will be made through the FDX Canada Council." FDX says its members are updating the global API spec to meet Canadian requirements, and that a "Canadian local profile" is "still to be finalized."
- Planned Canadian work areas: data formatting, a single security profile for participating entities, consent management, and error codes and status messaging.
- Upcoming discussion topics: consent and authorization, data element formatting, security, and conformance testing.
- The page gives no date for the finalized profile. It says requirements will go into the next version of the standard "in the months ahead," once Canadian regulations and guidance are final.
- **Not mentioned on the page:** Interac rails, TFSA, RRSP, FHSA, CAD, and French or bilingual requirements. [V]
- Account types in the regulations: Search results claimed the framework covers registered accounts such as TFSA and RRSP, with phasing for them. I could not confirm this from the June 2026 release or the Budget 2025 page, which name no account types. **[U]**

**What to watch:**
- FDX Canada Council announcements and the "Canadian local profile" page.
- The next FDX API version, and any Canadian section of it.
- Final Consumer-Driven Banking Regulations in the Canada Gazette, especially account types and phasing.
- Bank of Canada supervisory guidelines (consultation launched 2026-11-02).
- Any Interac or Payments Canada (Real-Time Rail) technical rules that the standard would reference.

### (b) Is FDX membership needed for the full data dictionary?

**Answer: Spec access does not require membership, per the FDX FAQ. Whether the data dictionary is gated is unverified.** [V] / [U]

- The FAQ says the API specification is "available on the FDX website without charge for parties that accept the terms and conditions" of the license. Access depends on accepting the license, not on membership. [V] https://financialdataexchange.org/about-fdx/faqs/
- The FAQ says "FDX membership also allows financial services stakeholders to participate in the FDX consortium," which implies membership is optional. [V]
- The developer portal is labelled "Developer Portal (Members Only)" in the FAQ page navigation. [V]
- The FAQ does not mention a data dictionary. Whether the data dictionary sits behind the members-only portal is `[U]`.
- Membership tiers and pricing are on a separate FDX Membership page. [V] (linked from the FAQ; not fetched here)
- The Tax-docs mirror says the FDX spec is "available upon request." That is the Tax docs, not the banking spec. [V] https://www.taxdataexchange.org/docs/fdx/v6.4.1/

**Practical reading for the demo:** Accept the spec licence to read the public FAQ-linked material. Ask FDX directly whether the data dictionary needs membership before depending on field-level detail.

## 5. Narrative for the demo

1. **Canadian governance of the standard.** FDX is the designated technical standards body, and the Canada Council makes Canada-specific decisions with voting limited to Canadian residents and businesses. That is the difference from using the US-run FDX spec as-is. [V]
2. **A Canadian supervisor, not just a standard.** The Bank of Canada supervises participating entities, accredited third-party providers, the standards body and the complaints body, and runs the public registry. A demo should show a registry check, not only an API call. [V]
3. **A Canadian complaints path.** OBSI is the external complaints body for unresolved consumer-driven banking complaints. [V]
4. **Accredited consent managers.** If a recipient outsources consent to an aggregator, that aggregator must be accredited, and the recipient stays liable. The consent screen can show who holds the consent. The accredited-provider badge is a demo proposal. [V] for the rule; proposal for the UI.
5. **Honest phasing.** Read access comes first, write access (payments) is targeted for legislation by mid-2027, and the screen-scraping ban applies only once the framework is fully operational. Say which of these the demo exercises. [V]
6. **Open Canadian gaps.** Canadian account types, CAD, French and Interac are not in the public v6 material. Label any such scope in the demo as a Canadian extension, not as part of FDX v6. [V] (the gap)
